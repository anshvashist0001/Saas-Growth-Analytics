📄 Executive Insights & Growth Strategy Roadmap
Projected Impact: +$240K ARR / -28% Churn
1. Product Onboarding Overhaul: The "Aha! Moment" Multi-Seat Initiative
Problem: 56.8% of users drop off before inviting a teammate. Solo users exhibit an 81.6% churn rate by day 90, whereas accounts with 2+ invited team members achieve 78.6% long-term retention (4.2x lift).
Strategic Action: Redesign first-session onboarding to require/incentivize team workspace sharing and automated template loading in under 5 minutes.
2. Proactive Customer Success Playbook Automation
Problem: Churn indicators manifest 20–30 days before cancellation (drop in 30-day login frequency < 3 days and unresolved support tickets).
Strategic Action: Integrate the composite Health Scoring SQL engine directly into Slack/CRM, triggering automatic CSM outreach whenever an account drops below a 50 Health Score.
3. Marketing Budget Re-allocation to High-LTV Channels
Problem: Google Paid Ads generate our lowest conversion rate (2.91%) and highest CAC ($890), yielding a marginal LTV/CAC of 2.10x. Conversely, Referral / Product Hunt yields a 6.20x LTV/CAC ratio.
Strategic Action: Reallocate 30% of paid ad budget into customer referral rewards and organic technical SEO documentation.
