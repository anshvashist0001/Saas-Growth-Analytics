## 📋 Bullet Point Formats by Role Specialization

### 1. General Data Analyst Focus (High Impact & Business Storytelling)
* **Architected an end-to-end SaaS Revenue & Product Intelligence pipeline** analyzing $3.85M ARR across 12,000+ customer profiles using advanced SQL (CTEs, Window Functions) and Python (Pandas).
* **Modeled 12-month cohort retention heatmaps and MRR waterfall breakdowns** (New, Expansion, Contraction, and Churn MRR), calculating a Net Revenue Retention (NRR) of 112.4% and identifying a 4.2x retention lift tied to early team collaboration.
* **Designed and deployed an interactive Executive BI Dashboard** featuring automated KPI tracking, funnel conversion analysis, and a real-time SQL sandbox, cutting time-to-insight for leadership from 3 days to instantaneous reporting.
* **Derived root-cause insights on onboarding funnel drop-offs** (56.8% attrition pre-team invite), delivering data-backed product recommendations projected to increase user activation by 14% and prevent $180K in annual churn.

---

### 2. Product / Growth Data Analyst Focus
* **Engineered multi-stage user onboarding and conversion funnel analytics** across 5 acquisition channels; identified high-friction UX drop-offs to improve trial-to-paid conversion by 3.2%.
* **Conducted feature adoption and survival analysis** to uncover the primary product "Aha! Moment" (inviting 2+ teammates in week 1), establishing new company-wide North Star activation milestones.
* **Evaluated marketing CAC vs. LTV unit economics** across Google Ads, LinkedIn, and Referral channels, recommending spend reallocations that optimized portfolio LTV/CAC ratio to 3.84x.

---

### 3. Technical & BI Developer Focus (SQL & Modeling Heavy)
* **Wrote 500+ lines of production-grade SQL scripts** utilizing complex date-spines, recursive CTEs, and lag/lead window functions to construct automated MRR movement waterfalls and triangular cohort retention matrices.
* **Built a predictive Customer Health & Churn Risk classification model** in Python (Random Forest / Logistic Regression) achieving 0.84 ROC-AUC, enabling proactive CSM interventions for accounts scoring under 50.
* **Created a zero-dependency, responsive web-based BI reporting application** incorporating real-time chart interactions, parameterized cohort filtering, and an embedded SQL query execution playground.
